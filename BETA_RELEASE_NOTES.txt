CapturePlay v0.9.0-beta.6

Beta update focused on the Multiview acceptance feedback.

- Multiview is now the single visible CapturePlay workspace while it is open. Common quality, display, recording, screenshot, fullscreen, mute and volume controls are available inside Multiview; the normal operator window remains hidden as the capture/control engine.
- Closing Multiview restores the same Primary native preview to the operator using the saved pre-handoff embed bounds, fixing the small-returned-preview regression.
- Replay Buffer now uses one Replay-only screen mirror of the physical display containing Multiview and crops the Primary tile. This can include the native child surface that the prior BrowserWindow-only mirror could omit. Replay still does not acquire the capture device again; 10s/30s share one bounded encoded ring and delayed audio remains off.
- Privacy cameras such as Surface Camera, webcams and StreamCam start stopped. App launch, entering Multiview and adding a camera Auxiliary do not begin camera capture until `カメラ開始` is pressed. Known capture cards such as GC575/GC510 keep automatic start.
- Native capture helper/C++ is unchanged.

Automated acceptance: Linux 457/457, unit 159/159; Windows 457/457, unit 159/159; package health and both direct v0.8.11 -> beta.6 and beta.5 -> beta.6 staged delta health PASS; native changes 0.

User acceptance remains required for real Primary handoff/restore, camera privacy start, and LIVE/10s/30s picture timing on the real capture environment.
